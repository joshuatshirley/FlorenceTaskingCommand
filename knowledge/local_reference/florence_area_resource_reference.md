# Florence, SC — Area Resource Reference

> Generic community reference for anyone relocating to or working in the Florence, SC area. De-personalized: derived from a family-relocation planning document, with every personal detail (individual preferences, specific family members, specific schools attended, personal checklists, job searches) removed. Intended as neutral "local knowledge" for conversational use — not advice specific to any individual or family.

## Schools

**Florence School District 1** — main phone (843) 679-7800, https://www.florenceschools.org/. Handles school assignment by address, enrollment, bus routes, lunch programs, special education and ELL services.

Elementary schools with strong local ratings include Royall Elementary, Lucy T. Davis Elementary, and Briggs Elementary (all K-5, small class sizes, strong academics per state ratings). John W. Moore Middle School (grades 6-8) is the highest-rated middle school in the area (top ~25th percentile statewide).

General school-rating lookup tools: Niche, GreatSchools, U.S. News Education, SchoolDigger, Carolina School Hub — all searchable by "Florence, SC."

## Parks & Outdoor Recreation

City of Florence Parks & Recreation: 14 parks, 600+ acres, 50 tennis courts, 25 soccer fields, 10 playgrounds, 5 community centers (https://www.cityofflorencesc.gov/parks-recreation-sports-tourism/recreation/).

Notable parks: **Timrod Park** (18 acres, central location, tennis/pickleball/amphitheater/trails), **Lynches River County Park** (676 acres between Florence and Lake City — canoe/kayak rentals, splash pad, climbing wall, archery), **Florence Rail Trail** (5-mile flat paved trail), **Jeffries Creek Park** (55 acres, creek trails, wetlands). The broader Florence trail system links 45+ miles of trails.

## Dining

A range of Asian cuisine options exist locally (Red Bowl Asian Bistro, New China Buffet, Lui's Chinese Restaurant, and several others) along with standard national chains. General search: "restaurants Florence SC" via Google Maps for current hours/reviews.

## Health & Medical

Carolinas Hospital System - Florence provides 24/7 emergency care. Multiple urgent care centers operate extended evening/weekend hours. Pediatric and dental care providers are typically found through referral (school district, other providers, or direct search).

## Churches / Faith Communities

Florence has an active church community across multiple denominations, with several large Baptist congregations (Florence Baptist Temple, South Florence Baptist Church, Ebenezer Baptist Church, First Free Will Baptist Church, Florence First Baptist Church) offering family/youth ministry programs. Most publish service times on their own websites.

## Utilities & Government

Utilities (electric/gas/water/internet) are set up through the City of Florence or the provider serving a specific address — Spectrum and AT&T both serve the area. SCDMV handles driver's license/vehicle registration address changes (required within 30 days of a move). Florence County Election Commission handles voter registration.

## Military & Community Support

Army OneSource (1-800-242-0123, https://www.armyonesource.army.mil/) provides free, confidential counseling and family-support resources Army-wide. Local Family Readiness Groups and Military Family Life Counselors are typically reached through the local recruiting station or Army community services.

## Community Connection Resources

Local Facebook groups ("Florence SC Moms," "Florence South Carolina Community," neighborhood/school-specific groups) and Nextdoor are commonly used for local recommendations and community news.

## Seasonal Notes

Fall brings school events, back-to-school activities, and (where available) fall festivals. Winter centers on holiday events. Spring brings community/school events and outdoor activity as weather warms. Summer is dominated by outdoor recreation (including the Lynches River splash pad) and summer camps/programs.
